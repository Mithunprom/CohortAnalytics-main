Privacy Policy

Effective 28 August 2026. Luma is made by Mithun Ghosh (mithunghosh404@gmail.com).

What we store

Luma keeps sparks, moods, scores, Rift Delve progress, and your Plus status on this device only (local storage). There is no Luma account and no Luma server in this version.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, health records, or advertising IDs. We do not run analytics SDKs, ads, or trackers. Mood check-ins never leave the phone and are comfort prompts, not medical data.

Payments

Apple processes Luma Plus through In-App Purchase. Apple’s privacy policy covers payment, Apple ID, and subscription management. Luma never sees your card number.

Kids

Luma is rated 12+. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not profile or track kids.
