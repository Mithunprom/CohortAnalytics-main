Luma Plus

Luma Plus is an auto-renewable Apple subscription.

  • Monthly: $4.99 USD, product app.luma.spark.plus.monthly
  • Yearly: $29.99 USD, product app.luma.spark.plus.yearly

Both include a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

What Plus is

Plus unlocks unlimited arcade energy, extra sparks, habitats/skins, and streak shields. Plus never sells mine power, rank, or an advantage over other players. Rank is earned in the Rift.

What Luma is not

Sparks and body prompts are not medical, therapy, or diagnostic advice. Kindness quests never require contacting strangers. There are no ads, loot boxes, or cash-outs to users.
